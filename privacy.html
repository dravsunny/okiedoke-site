Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Okiedoke ("we") is a small web design studio operated from New Jersey, USA. This policy explains what information we handle and why. Contact us at hello@okiedoke.design about anything here.

Information we collect

  • Public business information: to find businesses that might benefit from a new website, we look at publicly available sources such as business websites, directories and social media pages. We record business names, trades, locations, published phone numbers and published business email addresses, and public information about the services they offer.
  • Information you send us: emails, business details, photos, logos and website content you provide for a project.
  • Payment information: payments are handled by Stripe. We receive confirmation of payment and your billing name and email. We never receive or store full card numbers.
  • Website visitors: our website and concept pages don't use advertising trackers. Our hosting providers (GitHub and Cloudflare) may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To contact businesses about our services, using business email addresses they publish for enquiries. Every message includes a simple way to opt out.
  • To deliver projects and care plans, communicate with clients, and handle billing.
  • To prepare concept pages. These are unlisted previews, hidden from search engines, and we remove them on request.

We use AI services (currently OpenAI) to help research businesses, write and design pages, and draft and reply to email. Information needed for those tasks is sent to those services under their business terms. It is not used to train their models.

Opting out and your choices

Reply "no thanks" or "unsubscribe" to any email, or write to hello@okiedoke.design, and we'll stop contacting you and add you to a do-not-contact list. You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you or your business, and to remove a concept page made for your business.

Sharing

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers we use to run the business (Google Workspace for email, Stripe for payments, OpenAI for AI assistance, GitHub and Cloudflare for hosting), or where the law requires.

Storage and retention

Information is stored with the providers above, some of which are located outside your country, including in the United States. We keep outreach records, including do-not-contact lists, for as long as we're contacting businesses, and client records for as long as needed for the project, care plan, and tax and legal obligations.

Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change.
